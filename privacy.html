Luvra Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Luvra is a social app for sharing photos, videos and messages. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, Facebook or email, we receive your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Content you create: photos, videos, text posts, comments, reactions and chat messages you choose to post or send.
  • Usage information: basic activity such as when you were last online, so other people can see your presence.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account and show your profile and posts.
  • To deliver messages, comments and notifications.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent abuse and enforce our rules.

Sharing

Posts you publish can be seen by other users of the app. We do not sell your personal information. We use Supabase to store data and run sign-in.

Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used only when you use the camera feature. Media is processed on your device and uploaded only if you choose to post it.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, delete your posts, and ask us to delete your account and data by contacting us below.

Children

Luvra is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
